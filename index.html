About

Where the buildings come from

Buildings in the West End, Holmes-Foster/Highlands and College Heights are the Borough of State College's contributing properties in its two historic districts. The West End is the area HARB described on November 10, 2026, plus the Borough's draft West End zoning area. Downtown is the buildings on the 1982 Historic Resources of the Centre Region survey inside the draft Downtown zoning area, plus the Tavern. Demolished properties are shown but not counted.

Scans are taken from public sidewalks and alleys only, so sides between houses and backs not visible from an alley are not included. This page is a snapshot and is updated as work goes on. Building data is from Borough of State College GIS.

Heritage 3D LabThe Heritage 3D Lab in the Department of Architectural Engineering at Penn State uses 3D data and AI to understand, protect and reimagine older masonry buildings. Students in the lab will do the scanning, drone flights and Sanborn map work for this project. Visit the lab site.
