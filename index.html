Buildings in the West End, Holmes-Foster/Highlands and College Heights are the Borough of State College's contributing properties in its two historic districts. The West End is the area HARB described on November 10, 2026, plus the Borough's draft West End zoning area. Downtown is the buildings on the 1982 Historic Resources of the Centre Region survey inside the draft Downtown zoning area, plus the Tavern. Demolished properties are shown but not counted.

Scans are taken from public sidewalks and alleys only, so sides between houses and backs not visible from an alley are not included. This page is a snapshot and is updated as work goes on.

Data from Borough of State College GIS. Heritage 3D Lab, Department of Architectural Engineering, Penn State.
