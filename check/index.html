Sanborn Sheet Check

The index matches each historic building to the Sanborn fire insurance sheets that draw it (1906, 1911, 1922, 1929). These are the matches it was least sure of. Open each one, find the building on the sheet and say whether the sheet number is right.

Your checks are kept in this browser on this computer. At the end of each session, download them and drag the file into the sanborn-checks folder on the H3D-StateCollege Team.

How to check one
  • Open on the map. The public map opens on the building with that year's Sanborn map underneath. The building is outlined in red. Read the house number the Sanborn map prints on it, which is often different from today's.
  • Open the sheet. The library's viewer has a zoom. Find the same building on that sheet by its streets and printed number. Sheet 1 of each year has the key map and street index if you need to find the right sheet.
  • Right sheet if the sheet draws this lot. Wrong sheet if it's on another sheet, and type the right number. Not on any sheet if that year's maps stop short of the lot. Not sure if you can't tell, and say why in the note.
  • A lot cut by a sheet edge is on both sheets. Give both numbers, like 6 & 7.
