A 3D baseline of the West End

Walls and roofs for the – West End historic buildings, fitted to public lidar flown in March 2019. Each building has its measured wall height, roof shape and ridge height, without facade detail. Students will use it to plan the spring backpack scans and drone flights, and HARB has it as a record of the district before the rezoning.

    How the model was made

    Each building starts from the Borough of State College's building footprint, matched to the property in the HARB list. The roof comes from the US Geological Survey's 3D Elevation Program lidar for north central Pennsylvania, flown on March 28, 2019, before the leaves were out, at about six points per square metre. For every building we fit several simple roof shapes (flat, shed, gable, hip and cross-gable) to the lidar points inside the footprint and keep the simplest shape that fits nearly as well as the best one. Porches and one-storey additions are picked out by their lower height and modelled as separate flat blocks.

    Wall height is measured to the eaves of the main roof, and ridge height to its highest point, both from the ground beside the building. A building is marked as a good fit when most lidar points lie within half a metre of the model roof. Fair and poor fits usually have trees over the roof, dormers and wings that a simple shape leaves out, or a footprint that does not match what was there in 2019. Anything built or changed after March 2019 is not in the lidar, so the scans this spring will be the newer record.

    Building footprints and property data are from Borough of State College GIS. Lidar is USGS 3DEP, project PA_Northcentral_2019_B19. Aerial photos are © Nearmap. Sanborn maps are from the Penn State University Libraries' Donald W. Hamer Center for Maps and Geospatial Information. The plain map is the Esri light gray canvas (Esri, HERE, Garmin, © OpenStreetMap contributors). Facade photos on a few demo buildings are street-level imagery © Mapillary contributors, shared under CC BY-SA 4.0, and the textured walls are shared under the same licence. The photo used for each wall is listed when you select the building.
